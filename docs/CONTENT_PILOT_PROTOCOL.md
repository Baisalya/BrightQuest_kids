# BrightQuest Kids Supervised Pilot Protocol

This protocol is for product improvement, not for advertising an efficacy claim.

Use consented, supervised sessions appropriate to the child's age. Do not collect unnecessary identifying information. Use unseen pre items before learning and different unseen post items after learning. Record only the minimum evidence needed to understand usability and whether the intended skill changed.

For each class-pack pilot, observe at least five children for interaction/usability before calling the pack complete. Record confusing wording, reading load, touch/keyboard barriers, random guessing, hint usefulness and whether the child can explain the learned idea in a new context. Do not compare children publicly or create rankings.

Any small pilot result is internal evidence for improving BrightQuest. It must not be turned into claims such as “improves marks”, “guaranteed learning” or “board certified”.
