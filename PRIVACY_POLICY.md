# BrightQuest Kids — Plain-Language Privacy Policy (Release Draft)

BrightQuest Kids is designed to work offline and without advertising. The current app stores child profiles, learning progress, rewards, parent controls, diagnostic evidence, review schedules and learning preferences on the device.

The current product does not require a child to provide a real name, voice recording, location, contacts, advertising identifier, public profile, chat message or social post. BrightQuest does not include an advertising SDK or a social feed. Learning missions use local, pre-authored scenarios and do not upload child work.

Parent purchase controls are kept in the parent-gated area. A local purchase cache is not treated as proof of ownership for production class packs. Store verification must be completed through the relevant store or a future reviewed backend before paid content is unlocked.

No analytics or remote child-data collection should be enabled merely for product metrics. Any future remote analytics, account sync, cloud backup, voice capture, location feature or cross-platform entitlement account requires a separate child-privacy review, data-minimisation decision and consent design before release.

This is a release draft and must be checked against the actual shipping SDK list, Google Play Data Safety answers, Microsoft Store declarations, applicable child-privacy requirements and the final support/contact details before publication.
