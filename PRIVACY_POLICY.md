# BrightQuest Kids Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 14 September 2026
**Last updated:** 14 September 2026
**Developer:** Baishalya Roul (Baisalya)
**Website:** https://baisalya.com/brightquest-kids/
**Privacy URL:** https://baisalya.com/brightquest-kids/privacy.html
**Privacy/support email:** baishalya1999@gmail.com

## 1. Overview
BrightQuest Kids is designed as a local-first educational app for children and parents. The current app does not require an online child account, does not include an advertising SDK, and does not send a child’s learning activity to a BrightQuest analytics service.

## 2. Information handled locally
The app can store locally: learner name or nickname, avatar, stage/class selection, progress, mission state, mastery/review evidence, stars, rewards, achievements, cosmetic selections, healthy-play controls, learning goals, accessibility settings, audio/narration preferences, parent-lock state and app preferences such as version-specific “What’s New” dismissal.

Parents may use a nickname instead of a child’s legal name.

## 3. Collection, transmission and sharing
In the current release architecture, BrightQuest Kids does not transmit learner profiles, learning progress, parent settings or parent-lock data to a BrightQuest server. The developer does not operate a cloud child-profile database for the current app. BrightQuest Kids does not sell personal information or share child learning records with advertising, analytics or social-networking services.

## 4. Children’s privacy
The current product intentionally avoids child-facing advertising, public child profiles, social feeds, open/random chat, unnecessary sensitive permissions and online child-account creation. Parent-only controls and external links are kept in the grown-up area.

## 5. Device permissions and on-device services
The current Android release manifest does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone number, SMS, call logs, files/media or advertising-ID permissions. The app can query for a device text-to-speech service so lesson text can be spoken aloud. BrightQuest Kids does not record a child’s voice for narration.

## 6. External services
Parent-only links may open baisalya.com, email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Buy Me a Coffee, Google Play or Microsoft Store. Those destinations operate under their own privacy policies. BrightQuest Kids does not attach or send a child’s learning record when opening them.

## 7. Retention and deletion
Local app data remains on the device until it is removed using supported in-app controls, the operating system’s clear-storage control, or uninstall. Because the current app has no BrightQuest cloud child account, there is no remote BrightQuest learning-profile copy to request deletion from.

## 8. Security
BrightQuest Kids uses app-local and operating-system storage available to the device. The parent gate helps separate grown-up settings from learner use but does not replace the device lock screen or operating-system parental controls.

## 9. Purchases
The website does not currently represent BrightQuest Kids class packs as verified public paid products. If platform purchases are enabled later, payment processing will be handled by the applicable store or another clearly disclosed provider.

## 10. Changes
This policy will be updated when data practices materially change, including any future cloud account, sync, analytics, voice recording, location, remote AI, social feature or production billing that changes data handling.

## 11. Contact
Privacy questions can be sent to **baishalya1999@gmail.com** with the subject “BrightQuest Kids Privacy”. Do not send a child’s full legal name, parent PIN, recovery code or payment details.
